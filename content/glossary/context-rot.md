term: "Context rot"
aliases: ["context decay", "context degradation", "context poisoning"]
short_def: "The decline in an agent's answers as a session fills up and earlier material gets crowded out or summarized away. Mechanical, not fatigue."
not_to_be_confused_with: ["context-window"]
canonical_section: "e2-context-windows"
---

A session that started sharp and is now forgetting decisions has not gotten tired. It has run out of room and is working from a compressed version of what you told it. The fix is a fresh session with a short handoff note, not more reminders.
